## 2. PURPOSE AND OBJECTIVES

2.1 The Institution proposes to implement the I-WIN Pathway Program for eligible students.

2.2 The Program may include student profiling, career interest assessment, personality and attitudinal assessment, aptitude and capability assessment, communication skills assessment, academic learning, competitive examination preparation and career guidance.

2.3 Delivery of these services requires the collection, processing, storage, analysis and authorised disclosure of certain student personal information.

2.4 The purpose of this Agreement is to establish the responsibilities of both Parties in relation to the lawful, secure, transparent and responsible processing of such information.

2.5 The Parties recognise that children's educational records and assessment results require particular care and protection.

## 3. DEFINITIONS

For the purposes of this Agreement:

3.1 **Applicable Data Protection Law:** Means the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, applicable rules and notifications, and any other Indian laws governing the processing of personal information, to the extent in force and applicable.

3.2 **Personal Data:** Any data about an individual who is identifiable by or in relation to such data.

3.3 **Student Data:** Personal Data relating to students enrolled in the I-WIN Pathway Program, including registration, educational, assessment, performance and counselling information.

3.4 **Data Fiduciary:** A person who alone or in conjunction with others determines the purpose and means of processing Personal Data, as defined under applicable law.

3.5 **Data Processor:** A person who processes Personal Data on behalf of a Data Fiduciary.

3.6 **Processing:** Any wholly or partly automated operation performed on digital Personal Data, including collection, recording, storage, use, analysis, disclosure, retrieval, sharing, restriction or erasure.

3.7 **Data Principal:** The individual to whom the Personal Data relates. In relation to children, applicable statutory provisions concerning parents or lawful guardians shall apply.

3.8 **Authorised Users:** Persons permitted to access the I-WIN platform or Student Data for specified purposes.

3.9 **Personal Data Breach:** Any unauthorised processing or accidental disclosure, acquisition, sharing, use, alteration, destruction or loss of access to Personal Data that compromises its confidentiality, integrity or availability, as defined by applicable law.

3.10 **Sub-processor:** A third-party service provider engaged to process Personal Data on behalf of I-WIN in its capacity as a Data Processor.

## 4. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

4.1 The Parties shall identify their respective data protection roles for each processing activity in Schedule A.

4.2 Where the Institution determines the purposes and essential means of processing Student Data and instructs I-WIN to process such information solely on its behalf, the Institution shall act as Data Fiduciary and I-WIN shall act as Data Processor for that activity.

4.3 Where I-WIN independently determines the purposes and means of processing, including for separately authorised services, I-WIN may act as an independent Data Fiduciary for that activity.

4.4 Neither Party shall assume that its contractual designation automatically determines its statutory role. Actual processing responsibilities shall govern.

4.5 Each Party shall comply with the obligations applicable to its role under Applicable Data Protection Law.

4.6 Any independent processing purpose of I-WIN shall be identified in Schedule A and supported by the appropriate lawful basis, notice and consent or other applicable authorisation.

## 5. CATEGORIES OF STUDENT INFORMATION

Subject to the agreed program, Student Data may include:

### 5.1 Identification and Registration Information

- Student name and Student ID.
- Age, date of birth and class.
- School, section and academic year.
- Parent or guardian details.
- Contact information where required.

### 5.2 Student Profile Information

- Educational background.
- Career aspirations.
- Personal interests.
- Learning preferences.
- Extracurricular interests.
- Relevant educational support information.

### 5.3 Assessment Information

- Career interest responses and results.
- Personality and attitudinal assessment responses.
- Aptitude and capability assessment results.
- Communication skills assessment results.
- Academic competency assessments.
- Competitive examination practice and mock test scores.

### 5.4 Learning and Performance Information

- Learning resources accessed.
- Assignment completion.
- Practice test performance.
- Subject-wise knowledge gaps.
- Progress reports.
- Individual learning recommendations.

### 5.5 Counselling Information

Where included and authorised, relevant counselling records, guidance recommendations and educational development plans.

### 5.6 Technical Information

Account identifiers, authentication records, access logs and other information reasonably necessary for platform administration and security.

The Parties shall avoid collecting information unrelated to the specified educational purposes.

## 6. AUTHORISED PURPOSES OF PROCESSING

I-WIN may process Student Data for the purposes authorised under the Principal Agreement and Schedule A, including:

6.1 Registering students and managing authorised accounts.

6.2 Administering educational assessments.

6.3 Scoring assessment responses and generating student reports.

6.4 Identifying academic and developmental needs.

6.5 Providing personalised learning recommendations.

6.6 Supporting academic learning and competitive examination preparation.

6.7 Generating authorised student, parent, teacher and institutional dashboards.

6.8 Providing career guidance and counselling.

6.9 Monitoring program implementation and educational progress.

6.10 Maintaining platform functionality, security and technical support.

6.11 Complying with applicable legal obligations.

I-WIN shall not use Student Data for unrelated commercial purposes, sale to third parties or unauthorised advertising.

Any additional processing purpose shall require an appropriate lawful basis and documented authorisation.

## 7. INSTRUCTIONS FOR DATA PROCESSING

7.1 Where I-WIN acts as Data Processor, it shall process Student Data only on documented instructions from the Institution, except where processing is required by applicable law.

7.2 Documented instructions may be contained in the Principal Agreement, this DPA, its schedules or subsequent authorised written directions.

7.3 I-WIN shall promptly inform the Institution if it reasonably believes an instruction would violate Applicable Data Protection Law, unless prohibited from doing so by law.

7.4 Changes involving new categories of Student Data, new recipients or materially different processing purposes shall be documented and approved before implementation.

7.5 I-WIN shall not independently repurpose institution-controlled Student Data for unrelated product development, advertising, commercial profiling or third-party disclosure.

## 8. PARENTAL CONSENT AND STUDENT AUTHORISATION

8.1 The Parties recognise that many participating students will be below 18 years of age.

8.2 The Parties shall establish a documented process for providing appropriate privacy notices and obtaining verifiable parental or lawful guardian consent wherever required by Applicable Data Protection Law.

8.3 The responsibilities for issuing notices, verifying parental authority, obtaining consent, maintaining consent records and handling withdrawal shall be allocated in Schedule B.

8.4 The Institution shall not represent that general school admission consent or institutional enrolment automatically authorises all processing under the I-WIN Pathway Program.

8.5 I-WIN shall not commence processing that legally requires parental consent until the required authorisation has been established.

8.6 Each Party shall promptly communicate relevant consent withdrawals or changes in authorisation to the other Party.

8.7 The Parties shall implement appropriate measures to stop processing that no longer has a lawful basis, subject to legally permitted retention or other applicable exceptions.

## 9. CONFIDENTIALITY

9.1 Each Party shall maintain the confidentiality of Student Data received under this Agreement.

9.2 Access shall be restricted to authorised employees, counsellors, teachers, administrators and service providers who require the information for approved purposes.

9.3 Personnel with access to Student Data shall be subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations.

9.4 Neither Party shall disclose identifiable Student Data to unauthorised third parties.

9.5 Confidentiality obligations shall survive termination of this Agreement for as long as the information remains confidential or legally protected.

## 10. DATA SECURITY

10.1 I-WIN shall implement reasonable and appropriate technical and organisational security safeguards proportionate to the risks associated with the processing.

10.2 Such measures shall include, as appropriate:

- Secure authentication and access controls.
- Role-based permissions.
- Encryption or equivalent protective measures.
- Secure data transmission.
- Backup and recovery procedures.
- Security logging and monitoring.
- Vulnerability and patch management.
- Incident response procedures.
- Restricted administrative access.
- Appropriate personnel training.
- Security obligations for relevant service providers.

10.3 The Institution shall maintain appropriate safeguards for its own devices, accounts, networks and personnel accessing the platform.

10.4 Each Party shall promptly notify the other of suspected misuse of accounts or security weaknesses that may materially affect Student Data.

10.5 The specific security controls, hosting arrangements and responsibilities shall be documented in Schedule C.

## 11. ACCESS TO STUDENT REPORTS AND DASHBOARDS

11.1 I-WIN shall implement role-based access arrangements consistent with the agreed educational purposes.

11.2 Access may be provided as follows, subject to applicable law and authorisation:

- **Students:** Their own learning records, assessment findings and recommendations.
- **Parents / Lawful Guardians:** Relevant individual student reports and progress information.
- **Teachers:** Information reasonably necessary to support teaching, academic development and learning interventions.
- **School Counsellors:** Relevant assessment findings and career guidance information.
- **Principals:** Authorised institutional summaries and student-level information where necessary for legitimate educational responsibilities.
- **Government or Society Administrators:** Aggregated program dashboards and any specifically authorised student-level information necessary for approved administrative functions.

11.3 Access to detailed personality, attitudinal and counselling information shall be more restricted than access to routine academic performance information where appropriate.

11.4 Authorised users shall not download, distribute or disclose Student Data beyond their approved responsibilities.

11.5 The Institution shall promptly inform I-WIN when personnel access needs to be revoked or changed.

## 12. RESTRICTIONS ON THE USE OF ASSESSMENT INFORMATION

12.1 Student assessments are intended for educational and developmental purposes.

12.2 Assessment results shall not be treated as definitive measures of intelligence, personality, future success or professional suitability.

12.3 Neither Party shall use Student Data for unlawful discrimination, public labelling, punitive classification or exclusion from educational opportunities solely on the basis of automated assessment results.

12.4 Career-fit recommendations shall remain advisory.

12.5 Important educational and career decisions should involve appropriate human review and consideration of relevant contextual information.

12.6 Neither Party shall disclose identifiable student assessment results for publicity, testimonials or marketing without the necessary separate authorisation.

## 13. THIRD-PARTY SERVICE PROVIDERS AND SUB-PROCESSORS

13.1 I-WIN may engage authorised service providers for hosting, platform operations, payment processing, communications, technical support or other approved functions.

13.2 Where I-WIN acts as Data Processor, the engagement of Sub-processors shall be governed by the authorisation mechanism agreed in Schedule D.

13.3 I-WIN shall impose appropriate written data protection, confidentiality and security obligations on Sub-processors.

13.4 I-WIN shall remain responsible to the Institution for performance of its contractual processor obligations, including those delegated to Sub-processors.

13.5 Material changes to approved Sub-processors shall be communicated according to the notice and objection procedure specified in Schedule D.

13.6 I-WIN shall not permit Sub-processors to use Student Data for their own unrelated purposes.

## 14. INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS

14.1 The Parties shall document the principal hosting locations and any relevant international access or transfers in Schedule D.

14.2 International transfers shall comply with applicable Indian legal requirements and restrictions.

14.3 Where the Institution is subject to government procurement conditions or contractual data-location requirements, such conditions shall be expressly incorporated into the Principal Agreement or relevant schedule.

14.4 I-WIN shall not materially change an agreed data-location restriction without the required prior written authorisation.

## 15. DATA RETENTION, RETURN AND DELETION

15.1 Student Data shall be retained only for the agreed purposes and periods set out in Schedule E, subject to applicable law.

15.2 The Parties shall identify separate retention periods for registration information, assessment responses, reports, learning records, counselling records and technical logs.

15.3 Upon expiry or termination of the Principal Agreement, I-WIN shall, at the Institution's documented election and subject to applicable law:

- Provide an agreed export of institution-controlled Student Data.
- Return or securely delete such data.
- Delete remaining copies in accordance with agreed timelines.
- Retain only information that must lawfully be preserved or that I-WIN is independently authorised to retain.

15.4 Backup copies shall be handled under documented backup retention and deletion procedures.

15.5 Where requested and reasonably practicable, I-WIN shall provide written confirmation of completion of agreed deletion activities.

15.6 Each Party shall ensure that termination-related deletion does not unlawfully interfere with individual privacy rights or mandatory record-retention obligations.

## 16. PERSONAL DATA BREACH MANAGEMENT

16.1 Each Party shall maintain appropriate procedures for identifying and responding to Personal Data Breaches.

16.2 Where I-WIN becomes aware of a confirmed or reasonably suspected breach affecting institution-controlled Student Data, it shall notify the Institution without undue delay and, as a contractual target, within 24 hours of becoming aware of the incident.

16.3 The initial notification shall include available information regarding:

- Nature of the incident.
- Categories of information affected.
- Approximate number of affected individuals, where known.
- Potential consequences.
- Immediate containment measures.
- Contact details for incident coordination.

16.4 Information not available in the initial notification shall be provided as the investigation progresses.

16.5 The Parties shall cooperate in containment, investigation, remediation and required notifications.

16.6 Each Party shall fulfil any statutory breach notification obligations applicable to its role.

16.7 No provision of this Agreement shall delay or replace mandatory notifications to affected individuals or competent authorities.

16.8 The Parties shall preserve relevant incident records and cooperate in reasonable post-incident reviews.

## 17. DATA PRINCIPAL RIGHTS AND GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL

17.1 The Parties shall establish procedures for receiving and responding to requests relating to personal information.

17.2 Such requests may include access to prescribed information, correction, updating, erasure, withdrawal of consent and grievance redressal, as applicable.

17.3 Where I-WIN acts as Data Processor, it shall provide reasonable assistance to the Institution in responding to requests relating to institution-controlled Student Data.

17.4 If either Party receives a request primarily concerning the other's processing responsibilities, it shall refer or coordinate the request promptly, subject to lawful disclosure requirements.

17.5 Requests shall be handled within applicable statutory timelines.

17.6 Each Party shall designate an appropriate contact person for privacy matters.

## 18. AUDIT AND COMPLIANCE VERIFICATION

18.1 Upon reasonable written request, I-WIN shall provide information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with its obligations under this Agreement.

18.2 Such information may include relevant security policies, compliance summaries, independent assurance reports or other suitable evidence.

18.3 Any additional audit shall be subject to reasonable notice, confidentiality, security restrictions and arrangements that protect other customers' information.

18.4 Audits shall not require disclosure of unrelated confidential information, proprietary source code or information that would compromise platform security.

18.5 Where an audit identifies a material non-compliance, the Parties shall agree on a reasonable corrective action plan.

## 19. DATA OWNERSHIP AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

19.1 This Agreement does not transfer ownership of the Institution's pre-existing records or Student Data to I-WIN.

19.2 I-WIN retains its intellectual property rights in its software, assessment questionnaires, scoring methodologies, learning content, analytical models, report templates and related proprietary materials.

19.3 Individual students and other Data Principals retain their rights under applicable data protection law.

19.4 The Institution receives only those rights to access, use and retain reports and platform outputs expressly provided under the Principal Agreement.

19.5 I-WIN shall not claim unrestricted ownership of Student Data merely because such information is processed through its platform.

## 20. AGGREGATED DATA, RESEARCH AND ANALYTICS

20.1 I-WIN may generate aggregated educational performance information for purposes authorised under the Principal Agreement.

20.2 Aggregated reports may include:

- Program participation.
- Assessment completion.
- Subject-wise performance.
- Broad developmental trends.
- Learning progress.
- Program effectiveness indicators.

20.3 Public disclosure or independent research use of such information shall require appropriate authorisation and safeguards.

20.4 Information shall not be described as anonymous where individuals remain reasonably identifiable.

20.5 I-WIN shall not use identifiable institution-controlled Student Data to train external artificial intelligence models or develop unrelated commercial products without an appropriate lawful basis and express written authorisation.

## 21. INSTITUTIONAL RESPONSIBILITIES

The Institution shall:

21.1 Provide accurate student information through authorised channels.

21.2 Fulfil the notice and consent responsibilities allocated to it.

21.3 Nominate authorised administrators and users.

21.4 Maintain appropriate confidentiality and access controls.

21.5 Prevent unauthorised copying, downloading or disclosure of reports.

21.6 Promptly communicate account changes, consent withdrawals and relevant privacy requests.

21.7 Ensure that student assessment findings are used responsibly and for legitimate educational purposes.

21.8 Cooperate with I-WIN in resolving data quality, privacy and security issues.

## 22. I-WIN RESPONSIBILITIES

I-WIN shall:

22.1 Process Student Data only for authorised purposes.

22.2 Implement the agreed technical and organisational safeguards.

22.3 Maintain appropriate confidentiality controls.

22.4 Provide the contracted assessment, learning and reporting services.

22.5 Restrict access to authorised personnel and service providers.

22.6 Maintain procedures for security incidents and data breaches.

22.7 Assist with applicable data rights requests.

22.8 Implement agreed retention and deletion procedures.

22.9 Notify the Institution of material changes affecting agreed processing arrangements.

22.10 Cooperate in reasonable compliance verification.

## 23. LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION

23.1 Each Party shall be responsible for breaches of its own obligations under this Agreement and applicable law.

23.2 Each Party shall promptly take reasonable steps to mitigate loss arising from a breach for which it is responsible.

23.3 Any indemnification obligations, liability caps, exclusions and remedies shall be governed by the Principal Agreement, except where this Agreement expressly provides otherwise.

23.4 No contractual limitation shall exclude liability that cannot lawfully be excluded, including applicable statutory liabilities and remedies.

23.5 Neither Party shall be relieved of its legal responsibilities solely because the other Party has undertaken related contractual duties.

## 24. TERM AND TERMINATION

24.1 This Agreement shall take effect on the Effective Date and continue for as long as I-WIN processes Student Data under the Principal Agreement.

24.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement in accordance with the termination provisions of the Principal Agreement.

24.3 Where a material data protection breach occurs, the affected Party may require corrective action and may exercise applicable contractual termination rights.

24.4 Upon termination, the Parties shall comply with the data return, retention and deletion provisions of this Agreement.

24.5 Obligations relating to confidentiality, lawful retention, security, liability and cooperation shall survive termination to the extent necessary.

## 25. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

25.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India.

25.2 The Parties shall first attempt to resolve disputes through good-faith discussions between their authorised representatives.

25.3 If a dispute remains unresolved, the dispute resolution mechanism specified in the Principal Agreement shall apply.

25.4 In the absence of an agreed mechanism, competent courts at Hyderabad, Telangana, shall have jurisdiction, subject to mandatory statutory jurisdiction and applicable law.

## 26. ORDER OF PRECEDENCE

In the event of inconsistency:

1. Mandatory provisions of applicable law shall prevail.
2. This Agreement shall prevail over the Principal Agreement in relation to specific personal data processing obligations, unless the Parties expressly agree to a lawful alternative.
3. The Principal Agreement shall govern commercial, pricing, service delivery and other matters not specifically addressed here.

## 27. AMENDMENTS

Any material amendment to this Agreement shall be made in writing and approved by authorised representatives of both Parties.

Changes required by applicable law shall be implemented within the legally required period.
